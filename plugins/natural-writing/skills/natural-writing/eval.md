# Natural Writing evaluation

Run these checks before returning a diagnosis, edit, or draft. Fix every failed check that can be fixed without new information.

## Integrity

1. Every factual claim, name, number, date, quotation, citation, ranking, and material qualifier is supported by the source or the user.
2. The result preserves the original point, stance, uncertainty, and requested action.
3. Protected literals and formatting remain intact.
4. No invented anecdote, source, mechanism, opinion, or numerical specificity appears.

## Voice

5. The author would recognize the vocabulary, cadence, directness, humor, and level of polish.
6. Distinctive lines and useful rough edges survived unless they blocked comprehension.
7. The edit did not make every paragraph equally tidy, every sentence the same length, or every claim more corporate.
8. A supplied writing sample took priority over default punctuation and rhythm preferences.

## Quality

9. The opening earns its place and reaches the useful point at the right time for the channel.
10. Each paragraph adds a fact, mechanism, consequence, judgment, transition, or necessary context.
11. Actors and actions are clear; passive voice remains only when the actor is unknown, irrelevant, or deliberately backgrounded.
12. Abstract claims are supported by available specifics, not stronger invented ones.
13. Stock contrasts, fake insight, sales puffery, vague attribution, synonym cycling, canned transitions, and generic endings are absent or intentional.
14. Sentence and paragraph rhythm varies because the ideas vary, not because variation was mechanically injected.
15. Formatting follows the content; it does not decorate thin material.
16. Standalone prose contains no ritual validation, prompt restatement, coaching theater, invented objection, or unrequested offer to continue.
17. Creative prose modulates pace and earns figurative language instead of making every line vivid, explained, or profound.

## Mode contract

18. Diagnose mode names observable patterns and does not guess authorship or silently rewrite.
19. Edit mode returns the full edit plus a short material-change note unless file mode makes that redundant.
20. Draft mode stays within the brief and marks or asks for essential missing facts.
21. The response follows the requested format, audience, channel, and length.
22. The ending stops on the last useful point or requested action, without a generic flourish or offer to continue.
