## Decisions

- **Adopt:** a hybrid-page example in the existing taxonomy, preserving the route from
  spatial entrance to usable task. No new surface, broad lead or mandatory 3D stack.
- **Adopt conditionally:** sound/media interaction checks under the existing runtime
  quality owner. This is our design synthesis for applicable products, not a claim
  that the source authors specified or demonstrated every check.
- **Adopt:** concrete question examples for purpose, navigation, intentional sound
  entry and physical use. Ask only for unresolved choices that change implementation;
  reuse prior answers and continue independent authorized work.
- **Already covered:** one lead, source/adaptation/implementation labels, accepted
  palette preservation, bounded previews, motion inspection, licensing and full-flow
  checks. Keep these rules rather than adding another broad design ritual.
- **Reference only:** the particular shelf/tour aesthetic, photoreal asset claims and
  model efficiency claims. No third-party code, assets, paid service or optional skill
  is installed by this release.

## Validation

The manual cases in [reference-steering-cases.md](../tests/reference-steering-cases.md)
cover applicable and nearby non-trigger cases, settled intent and autonomous work.
Package checks, local Markdown links, manifest/version alignment and field-guide data
parity are checked separately. They establish package consistency, not improved design
quality, live source behavior or success on a physical tablet.
