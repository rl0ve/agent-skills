# Changelog

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-21

- Make visual selection a completion check, with a short project-local decision record
  and verification that selected explanations reached the actual artifact.
- Require a concrete reason for prose-only treatment of a hard-to-follow central
  relationship, handoff or sequence, and an included/omitted learning-value decision
  when a motion overview was invited. Keep selection conditional rather than a quota.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

- Add a research-synthesis entry point distinct from issue-by-issue assessments.
- Preserve primary evidence, attribution, quantitative scope, counterarguments and
  claim IDs across a report and necessary derivatives.
- Deliberately select the few diagrams, charts or captures that explain the central
  findings; treat cards and navigation as presentation rather than visual explanation.
- Gate a short HyperFrames-style overview on added learning value and a static
  alternative; distinguish conceptual motion from recorded UI walkthroughs.
- Default to one primary document format; DOCX and PDF together only when requested.
- Inventory and update/supersede existing research assets during a continuation.
