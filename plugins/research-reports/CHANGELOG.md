# Changelog

## 1.0.4 - 2026-10-22

- Carry saved writing and formatting preferences into the report's reading contract.
- Preserve essential current-state mechanisms when condensing a decision brief.
- Make proposed workflow changes explicit about location, trigger, owner, timing and
  required versus optional capture, using short labels with necessary definitions.
- Use bullets and comparison tables where they reduce reading work without replacing
  the reasoning that connects the recommendations.

## 1.0.3 — 2026-10-22

- Route durable quantitative figures and in-chat exploration to available specialists
  while preserving the selected surface and research depth.
- Add optional standalone HTML renderer guidance for Observable Plot, Vega-Lite,
  ECharts, Plotly and D3, with delivered/exported verification.
- Keep ImageGen optional for conceptual illustration, with exact data and structure
  in editable or code-rendered graphics.

## 1.0.2 — 2026-10-21

- Check broad strategy and audience coverage before framing a company-wide question
  through one product; retain uncertainty about recalled sources.
- Distinguish actual query/access coverage, populated persona fields and identity joins
  from schemas, saved extracts and account-level overlap.
- Require a final prose review, honor requested writing guidance and distinguish it
  from a planned pass or pattern linter.
- Keep short evidence visible in reading HTML; select interaction for a concrete
  reader benefit and inspect text, labels and print styles at reading size.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-21

- Make visual selection a completion check, with a short project-local decision record
  and verification that selected explanations reached the actual artifact.
- Require a concrete reason for prose-only treatment of a hard-to-follow central
  relationship, handoff or sequence, and an included/omitted learning-value decision
  when a motion overview was invited. Keep selection conditional rather than a quota.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-21

- Add a research-synthesis entry point distinct from issue-by-issue assessments.
- Preserve primary evidence, attribution, quantitative scope, counterarguments and
  claim IDs across a report and necessary derivatives.
- Deliberately select the few diagrams, charts or captures that explain the central
  findings; treat cards and navigation as presentation rather than visual explanation.
- Gate a short HyperFrames-style overview on added learning value and a static
  alternative; distinguish conceptual motion from recorded UI walkthroughs.
- Default to one primary document format; DOCX and PDF together only when requested.
- Inventory and update/supersede existing research assets during a continuation.
