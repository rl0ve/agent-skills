# Audience directives

Read only the selected section.

## b2b-saas

- Preserve useful density; premium does not mean inflated whitespace.
- Make loading, empty, error, success, disabled, focus, hover, selection, and partial states explicit.
- Support keyboard navigation, shortcuts, tables, filters, and dark-mode parity where relevant.
- Prefer restrained motion and product evidence over decorative imagery.
- Default mode: `Operate`, plus `Read` for documentation.

## b2c-ecommerce

- Let product media lead; place reviews, guarantees, shipping, returns, and payment trust near decisions.
- Design mobile first with reachable controls, persistent cart state, and clear variants/inventory.
- Use urgency only when it is true and attributable.
- Treat cart, checkout, and errors as product flows, not marketing sections.
- Default modes: `Persuade` plus `Operate`.

## b2c-consumer-app

- Welcome personality, direct manipulation, gesture-aware behavior, and contemporary interaction patterns.
- Keep first-run value immediate; explain through use rather than dense instruction.
- Use motion for orientation and feedback, with reduced-motion parity.
- Default modes: `Experience` plus `Operate`.

## b2b-marketing

- Make the category and value understandable in five seconds.
- Lead with specific proof: customer outcomes, real names where public, security/compliance, and implementation reality.
- Optimize for executive scanning without deleting technical substance.
- Use restrained motion unless the product itself sells creative or developer craft.
- Default modes: `Persuade` plus `Read`.

## b2c-marketing

- Let brand voice, imagery, emotion, and editorial composition carry the pitch.
- Keep mobile performance and legibility intact when expression increases.
- Prefer memorable structure to a longer feature list.
- Default modes: `Persuade` plus `Experience`.

## prosumer-tool

- Combine expert density with visible craft.
- Make keyboard-first operation, command palettes, shortcuts, and thoughtful empty states first-class.
- Use subtle motion and expressive detail without reducing throughput.
- Default modes: `Operate` plus restrained `Experience`.

## internal-tool

- Optimize throughput, predictability, and training cost.
- Accept high density when it improves operational scanning.
- Refuse unrequested creative effects and motion budgets.
- Default mode: `Operate` only.

## content-editorial

- Treat typography, measure, hierarchy, navigation, citations, and reading rhythm as the design.
- Keep body measure near 60–75 characters and preserve deep links/semantic structure.
- Expression may support the subject but must not interrupt comprehension.
- Default mode: `Read`.

## portfolio-personal

- Prioritize a distinctive point of view and memorable work presentation.
- Use motion when it reveals process or authorship, not as decorative delay.
- Keep contact and hiring paths obvious despite expressive composition.
- Default modes: `Experience` plus `Persuade`.
